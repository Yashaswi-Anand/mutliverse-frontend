Two accounts. One phone.

Multiverse runs a second copy of an app alongside the first — its own storage, its own login, its own notifications. Work and personal, without signing out.

What it does

⧉

Real second copies

Each clone gets its own private storage. Two logins to the same service can be open at once, and neither one signs the other out.

🔔

Notifications still work

A clone can notify you like any other app — a second messenger you have to keep opening manually is not worth installing.

🔒

App lock

Fingerprint or a PIN in front of the whole app. The PIN is never stored — only a salted hash of it, which cannot be reversed.

✎

Name them yourself

Rename and reorder your clones so the list reads the way you think — "Work", "Personal", a person's name — not "App 2".

⃠

No account, no ads

No sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. There is nothing to log in to, because there is no server.

☾

Light and dark

Follows your system theme, or pin it to light or dark from Settings.

Worth knowing before you install

Cloning is not magic, and it is fairer to say so here than to let you find out afterwards.

  • Apps that insist on Google Play services may not run. Some apps — banking and trading ones in particular — check for Play services and refuse to start without them.
  • "Sign in with Google" generally will not work inside a clone. That check happens on Google's servers, not on your phone, so there is nothing an app on your side can do about it.
  • Some apps simply resist being cloned. A few detect the sandbox and stop; others behave oddly in it. If one misbehaves, tell us from Settings → Report a bug and it can be looked into.
  • Permissions belong to the clones, not to Multiverse. A cloned app can only use the camera, microphone or contacts if Multiverse holds that permission — so Multiverse asks for it on the clone's behalf. It never uses them itself. The privacy policy spells this out.

Found a problem?

There is a bug report form inside the app — Settings → Report a bug. You can attach screenshots, and it sends straight to the developer. Or write to anandyash1711@gmail.com.
