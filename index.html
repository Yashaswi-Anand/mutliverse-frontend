Features

What it does

Real separate copies

Every clone gets its own private storage. Several logins to the same service stay open at once, and none of them signs the others out.

Notifications still work

A clone can notify you like any other app. A second messenger you have to keep opening by hand is not worth installing.

App lock

Fingerprint or a PIN in front of the whole app. The PIN is never stored — only a salted hash of it, which cannot be reversed.

Name them yourself

Rename and reorder clones so the list reads the way you think — "Work", "Personal", a person's name — instead of "App 2".

No account, no ads

No sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. There is nothing to log in to, because there is no server.

Light and dark

Follows your system theme, or pin it from Settings — the same choice this page gives you in the corner above.

Before you install

Worth knowing

Cloning is not magic, and it is fairer to say so here than to let you find out afterwards.

Apps that insist on Google Play services may not run. Some apps — banking and trading ones in particular — check for Play services and refuse to start without them.

"Sign in with Google" generally will not work in a clone. That check happens on Google's servers, not on your phone, so there is nothing an app on your side can do about it.

Some apps simply resist being cloned. A few detect the sandbox and stop; others behave oddly inside it. If one misbehaves, tell us from Settings → Report a bug.

Some apps ask not to be cloned, and we listen. An app can declare that it will not run inside a container. Those apps are left out of the list, and Multiverse will not open a clone of one. There is no override.

Permissions belong to the clones, not to Multiverse. A cloned app can only use the camera or your contacts if Multiverse holds that permission — so Multiverse asks on the clone's behalf, and never uses them itself. The microphone is not requested at all. The privacy policy spells this out.

Support

Found a problem?

There is a bug report form inside the app — Settings → Report a bug. You can attach screenshots, and it sends straight to the developer. Or write to contact@socialriser.com.
